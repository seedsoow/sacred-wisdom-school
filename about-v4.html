Who she is
"This school exists because I lived it. Everything I teach began as something I needed to learn." — Rain

Reverend Dr. Rain Lim is an international spiritual teacher, intuitive healer, speaker, and transformational guide dedicated to awakening the deeper wisdom within humanity.

Her work bridges spirituality, healing, and personal transformation — guiding individuals to reconnect with their inner truth and live from a place of authenticity, compassion, and conscious awareness.

Through thousands of individuals guided across Asia, Canada, and beyond, Rain's teachings have reached people at every stage of the spiritual journey — from those taking their first step inward to advanced practitioners deepening decades of practice.

A Journey of Healing and Awakening

A path that began long before there were words for it.

Rain's sensitivity began in childhood. She was eight or nine years old, living above her family's coffee shop in town, when sounds started coming at night — car doors opening and closing in the empty street below. The sounds came for several nights. When she finally checked, nothing was there. She slept in her parents' bed for a week. Her father's family — aunts and uncles who served as mediums, channeling for the family — meant this world was not entirely foreign, even when young Rain had no words for what she was experiencing.

From 2006 she committed to a deeper healing journey — working through her own history, her emotional patterns, the wounds she had been carrying. That sustained work cleared the ground within her. She believes it made her a clearer vessel, which is why in 2013 something could open so much wider.

That year her sensitivity expanded in a way that called her fully forward. Presences, energetic fields, a knowing beneath ordinary life — she wanted to understand what was happening. She entered a ministry program and found two teachers who shaped her profoundly. Jyoti carries the living wisdom of our indigenous peoples — ancient, ceremony-rooted, earth-based. Penny Kelly bridges science and galactic experience. Both contributed to who Rain became.

During those years of formation, Thoth arrived — the Egyptian keeper of sacred knowledge. Through that connection the healing modalities now at the heart of Sacred Wisdom School came to Rain in direct transmission. Kuan Yin and Isis arrived not long after — both have continued to support Rain's work ever since.

In 2018 Rain traveled to Nepal on spiritual pilgrimage. She sat with Amma Bumbo, one of the 13 Grandmothers — wisdom keepers from indigenous traditions across the world. What arrived in that healing space was pure grace: a cacao seed blessed under moonlight by a second Grandmother, carried from Mexico, placed in Rain's hands at the exact moment of ceremony. Two Grandmothers on the same day. Something that arrives like that changes a person.

The years that followed brought many more spiritual experiences — sacred plant medicine ceremonies, healings witnessed and received, visions, encounters that deepened every layer of her gifts. In Hawaii she entered a cacao ceremony held by indigenous elders, received a vision of a past life, and left with a clear message: she would one day hold that ceremony herself. The Phoenix Oracle Deck and the Atlantean deck came later — more transmissions, more wisdom brought through and offered to the world.

Sacred Wisdom School exists as the fruit of this journey — and as the space Rain wishes she had found at the beginning of it.

Awakening Intuition and Spiritual Awareness

A profound awareness of the emotional and energetic patterns that shape human experience.

As Rain continued exploring healing and consciousness, her intuitive sensitivity deepened. She developed a profound awareness of emotional and energetic patterns that influence human experience.

Over time, she began guiding others through healing journeys that addressed the mind, the body, the emotions, and the subtle energy fields.

Her approach integrates intuitive guidance with a grounded understanding of human psychology, emotional healing, and spiritual growth.

Through her teachings and healing work, thousands of individuals have experienced profound shifts in awareness, emotional freedom, and personal transformation.

A Bridge Between Spirituality and Everyday Life

Spiritual wisdom in service of real, lived transformation.

In addition to her spiritual work, Rain holds a Ph.D. in Entrepreneurship and has built and led several successful businesses. She is the founder of Origin of Wisdom, where she continues to guide internationally recognized transformational programs including SEEDS Leadership and the Facilitator Development Program.

Her unique background allows her to bridge spiritual wisdom with practical understanding of leadership, personal transformation, and conscious living. These programs continue to reach participants across multiple countries.

At the centre of Rain's teachings

True transformation begins when we remember who we truly are.

Beneath the roles we play and the identities we carry, there exists a deeper awareness — a quiet wisdom that has always been present. Through Sacred Wisdom School, Rain continues guiding individuals on the path of healing, awakening, and spiritual discovery.
