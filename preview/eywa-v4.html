Eywa

Partner & Faculty  ·  Intuitive Guide  ·  Energy Healer  ·  Tarot Practitioner  ·  Spiritual Facilitator

Eywa's work is rooted in self-remembrance — the journey of reconnecting with who we are beneath conditioning and learning to live with greater authenticity, clarity, and self-trust.

Her spiritual path began through her own healing journey. During a period of anxiety and emotional overwhelm, she reached a turning point that invited her to begin finding her way home to herself.

Within the community of Sacred Wisdom School, Eywa gradually rediscovered what brought her joy, what felt true to her, and what her heart was calling her towards. This experience continues to shape the way she holds space for others — with compassion, sensitivity, honesty, and grounded presence.

Through intuitive guidance, Tarot, energy healing, ceremonies, and workshops, Eywa creates experiential spaces where individuals can deepen their relationship with themselves and reconnect with their inner wisdom.

Her Role at Sacred Wisdom School

As a partner and faculty member of Sacred Wisdom School, Eywa contributes to the growth and development of the school while supporting students in deepening self-awareness, strengthening intuition, and bringing spiritual wisdom into everyday life. She believes meaningful transformation begins through a deeper relationship with ourselves.
