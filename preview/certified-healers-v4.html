Become a Certified Healer

Sacred Wisdom School trains and certifies healers who carry the depth, care, and integrity that this work requires. The journey begins with the Foundation Program — and deepens across each level of the healing path.

Graduates who complete certification are invited to be listed in the Sacred Wisdom School Certified Healers directory and to join the growing community of practitioners carrying this work into the world.

Begin with Level 1 Contact Us
  • 1
    Complete the Foundation Program (Level 1) The entry point for all Sacred Wisdom School students — covering the essential principles of holistic healing and six healing modalities.
  • 2
    Deepen Through Levels 2 and 3 Expand your healing practice through Inner Child Healing and the three Advanced Modules — Sacred Breathwork, Sacred Spirit Dance, and Sacred Integrative Constellations.
  • 3
    Complete Certification Requirements Certification requirements are shared with students progressing through the programs. Your journey as a student is also your training as a healer.
  • 4
    Join the Certified Healers Directory Certified graduates are invited to be listed here and to join the Sacred Wisdom School community of practitioners — carrying this work forward with integrity.
