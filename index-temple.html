The quiet question

Many spiritual journeys begin with a question that will not go away.

Why do certain patterns repeat in my life? What is the deeper purpose of my existence? Is there more to life than what I can see on the surface?

These questions often arise during change, challenge, or reflection. They are invitations to look inward, listen more deeply, and reconnect with the wisdom that already lives within.
