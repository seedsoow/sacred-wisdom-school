Ichi Lim

Partner & Faculty  ·  Holistic Healer  ·  Spirit Channeler  ·  Transformational Life Coach  ·  Founder, Ichi Sanctuary

Ichi is a deeply intuitive and compassionate healer devoted to guiding others back to themselves — a place of inner safety, connection, wholeness, and freedom.

Through her work with individuals around the world, she supports healing and transformation from within. Her gentle presence carries a quiet strength, while her deep connection with Spirit allows her to perceive what may lie beneath the visible layers of a person's experience.

As the founder of Ichi Sanctuary, she integrates spirituality, inner work, energy healing, and transformational coaching. Her work supports individuals in developing greater clarity, emotional resilience, self-trust, and a deeper relationship with their inner guidance.

Ichi believes every person carries the wisdom and capacity to create a life that reflects who they truly are. She creates grounded and respectful spaces rooted in safety, discernment, personal responsibility, and compassionate presence.

Her Role at Sacred Wisdom School

As a partner and faculty member of Sacred Wisdom School, Ichi contributes to the growth and development of the school while guiding students in deepening self-awareness, developing spiritual discernment, strengthening intuitive capacity, and applying their gifts with integrity. Her teaching encourages students to build trust in themselves and embody their spiritual understanding through authentic, purposeful, and compassionate living.
