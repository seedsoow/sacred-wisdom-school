Sacred Healing Program — Advanced Level

Sacred Breathwork · Sacred Spirit Dance · Sacred Integrative Constellations

At the Sacred Healing Program Advanced Level, participants embark on a transformative journey into three sacred arts of healing and awakening. These are offered as a harmonious package — three interconnected pathways that together support the deepening of spiritual growth, emotional resilience, and holistic well-being.

This path works directly with the body and the emotions. Participants release what has been held for years, see ancestral patterns clearly for the first time, and recover a sense of wholeness that the ordinary world rarely allows.

Module One

Sacred Breathwork

The body knows how to heal. Breath creates the conditions.

Sacred Breathwork is a powerful inner journey that uses conscious breathing, sound, and somatic awareness to activate the body's natural healing intelligence. Breath is one of the most direct bridges between the body, mind, and emotions.

Through intentional breathing patterns combined with sound and guided awareness, participants enter a deeper state of consciousness where the body can release tension, emotions, and energetic blockages that may have been held for many years. In this expanded state of awareness, the body begins to restore balance and release what it has been holding.

Sacred Breathwork creates a safe and supportive space where participants reconnect with the intelligence of their own body and allow healing and insight to arise naturally from within.

·

Voice and Body Together

Integration of breath, vocal toning, and somatic awareness — the voice becomes a tool for release and expression.

·

Body-Based Release

Stored emotions and tension move through the body, beyond the reach of the mind alone.

·

Gentle yet Powerful

A gentle yet powerful approach that honors the natural pace of each individual.

·

Spiritual Deepening

Often deepens connection with inner wisdom beyond emotional release alone.

Module Two

Sacred Spirit Dance

Long before words, there was movement. The body has always known the way.

Sacred Spirit Dance is a movement-based healing practice that invites participants to reconnect with the intelligence of the body through music, rhythm, and intuitive expression. The body carries memories, emotions, and experiences sometimes beyond words.

Sacred Spirit Dance invites intuitive movement — the body expressing itself naturally, free of choreography or performance. Through music, breath, and sustained movement, participants may naturally enter expanded states of consciousness — trance-like states where the mind becomes quieter and the body moves with greater freedom and intuition, allowing emotions, insights, and inner experiences to surface.

All bodies are welcome. Participants are invited to move in the way that feels natural for them.

·

Intuitive Movement

The body expresses itself naturally, without choreography or performance.

·

Emotional Release

Energetic release through movement held in a safe and supportive container.

·

Music as Healer

Music guides emotional expression and deeper awareness throughout the journey.

·

Expanded States

Access to expanded states of consciousness through rhythm and sustained movement.

Module Three

Sacred Integrative Constellations

We arrive in this life connected — to family, to lineage, to those who came before. What has been carried can be set down.

Sacred Integrative Constellations is a powerful healing modality that reveals and transforms hidden dynamics within family systems, ancestral lineages, and unconscious emotional patterns. Many of the challenges people experience in relationships, emotional well-being, and life decisions are connected to unresolved dynamics within the wider family system.

Events such as loss, trauma, exclusion, or unresolved conflicts within a family system can echo through generations, influencing how individuals experience life today. Sacred Integrative Constellations creates a space where these deeper systemic influences can be observed and understood. By acknowledging what has been unseen or unspoken within a family system, the natural movement toward balance and healing can begin.

·

Expanding the Foundation

Inspired by the foundational work of Bert Hellinger, expanded by Dr. Rain Lim through emotional release and somatic awareness.

·

Body and Field Together

Participants understand the dynamics within their system and release the emotional and energetic imprints held in the body.

·

Ancestral Exploration

Space for ancestral influences, past life experiences, health patterns, and multidimensional aspects of the self.

·

Safe and Held

All explorations are approached with openness, curiosity, and a deeply held sense of safety.
