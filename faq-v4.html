About the School

Sacred Wisdom School is a sanctuary for healing, spiritual teachings, and conscious living, founded by Reverend Dr. Rain Lim. It offers a progressive path of sacred healing programs, spiritual teachings, and transformational journeys — in Malaysia, Canada, and online.

Reverend Dr. Rain Lim is an international spiritual teacher, intuitive healer, speaker, and the founder of Sacred Wisdom School. She holds a Ph.D. in Entrepreneurship and has guided thousands of individuals across Asia, Canada, and beyond on their healing and spiritual journeys.

Her path began in childhood and has included decades of spiritual study, pilgrimage, sacred plant medicine ceremonies, and direct transmission — from teachers including Jyoti and Penny Kelly, and through direct connection with Thoth, Kuan Yin, and Isis.

No. Sacred Wisdom School is not affiliated with any religion or belief system. Participants come from all backgrounds and all faiths — or none. The teachings and healing work honor the deeper wisdom present in all traditions while belonging to none.

Programs are primarily offered in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur) and Canada. Selected programs are available in online formats. Sacred Journeys are held internationally — including Bali, Malaysia, and Nepal.

The Programs

Sacred Wisdom School offers three pillars of programs: Sacred Teachings (exploring consciousness, wisdom, and spiritual development), Sacred Healing (a three-level progressive healing path), and Sacred Journeys (transformational retreats and spiritual travel).

No prior experience is needed to begin. The Foundation Program (Level 1) is designed for those at the very beginning of their healing or spiritual journey — as well as for experienced practitioners looking to deepen their understanding. All are welcome.

Level 1 (Foundation Program) introduces the essential principles of holistic healing and six healing modalities — it is the entry point for all students. Level 2 (Inner Child Healing) is a deeper journey into the emotional patterns formed in childhood. Level 3 (Advanced Modules) introduces three sacred healing arts: Sacred Breathwork, Sacred Spirit Dance, and Sacred Integrative Constellations. Level 2 and 3 both require completion of Level 1.

Yes. Level 3 requires only the Foundation Program (Level 1) as a prerequisite. Level 2 and Level 3 can be taken in either order after completing Level 1.

All Sacred Wisdom School programs are offered in intentionally small, intimate group settings. This is a deliberate choice — the depth of the work requires a safe, contained space that larger groups cannot provide.

The Experience

Programs at Sacred Wisdom School are deeply experiential — participants learn through guided healing practices, direct experience, and reflection, not through theory alone. You can expect a safe, sacred group environment; a combination of teaching, guided healing, personal exploration, and integration time; and a community of fellow students walking a similar path.

There is no obligation to share beyond what you are comfortable with. Sacred Wisdom School programs honor each person's process and pace. The group environment is one of mutual respect and care.

Sacred Breathwork uses conscious breathing, sound, and somatic awareness to activate the body's natural healing intelligence. Sacred Spirit Dance is a movement-based healing practice that invites intuitive, free movement guided by music — the body expressing its own wisdom. Sacred Integrative Constellations reveals and transforms hidden dynamics within family systems and ancestral lineages. Each has its own dedicated page with full information.

Sacred Journeys

Sacred Journeys are transformational retreats and spiritual travel experiences guided by Dr. Rain Lim — to destinations including Bali, Malaysia, and Nepal. They combine sacred ceremony, healing practices, pilgrimage, and community in a space that steps entirely outside the rhythms of everyday life.

Some Sacred Journeys are open to all, while others are offered in connection with the healing programs. Contact us with your specific interest and we will guide you to the right option.

Journey dates are announced throughout the year. The Malaysia Sacred Journey is currently open for enquiries. Bali and Nepal dates will be announced soon. Register your interest on the Sacred Journeys page to be notified first.
