Founder — Dr. Rain Lim

Reverend Dr. · International Spiritual Teacher · Intuitive Healer · Founder, Sacred Wisdom School

Reverend Dr. Rain Lim is an international spiritual teacher, intuitive healer, speaker, and transformational guide dedicated to awakening the deeper wisdom within humanity. Her work bridges spirituality, healing, and personal transformation — guiding individuals to reconnect with their inner truth and live from a place of authenticity, compassion, and conscious awareness.

Through thousands of individuals guided across Asia, Canada, and beyond, Rain's teachings have reached people at every stage of the spiritual journey — from those taking their first step inward to advanced practitioners deepening decades of practice.

A Journey of Healing and Awakening

Rain's path into spiritual teaching began through her own healing journey. Through confronting childhood trauma and navigating profound personal challenges, she discovered the deep relationship between emotional healing and spiritual awakening. She realized that transformation arises from more than intellectual understanding alone.

True healing emerges when individuals are willing to look inward and reconnect with the deeper intelligence within themselves. This realization became the foundation of everything she teaches.

Awakening Intuition and Spiritual Awareness

A profound awareness of the emotional and energetic patterns that shape human experience.

As Rain continued exploring healing and consciousness, her intuitive sensitivity deepened. She developed a profound awareness of emotional and energetic patterns that influence human experience. Over time, she began guiding others through healing journeys that addressed the mind, the body, the emotions, and the subtle energy fields.

Her approach integrates intuitive guidance with a grounded understanding of human psychology, emotional healing, and spiritual growth. Through her teachings and healing work, thousands of individuals have experienced profound shifts in awareness, emotional freedom, and personal transformation.

The Work

Integrating spiritual wisdom and human understanding across many disciplines.

  • Emotional healing and inner child work
  • Energy healing, chakra awareness, sound healing
  • Sacred Rainbow Energy Healing — a unique modality received by Rain through spiritual inspiration
  • Sacred Breathwork and Sacred Spirit Dance — somatic and movement-based healing modalities created by Rain
  • Sacred Integrative Constellations — expanding the foundational work of Bert Hellinger
  • Intuitive development and spiritual guidance

She integrates these approaches into a holistic understanding of human transformation. Healing and spiritual awakening, in her teaching, are deeply interconnected paths.

A Bridge Between Spirituality and Everyday Life

Spiritual wisdom in service of real, lived transformation.

In addition to her spiritual work, Rain holds a Ph.D. in Entrepreneurship and has built and led several successful businesses. She is the founder of Origin of Wisdom, where she continues to guide internationally recognized transformational programs including SEEDS Leadership and the Facilitator Development Program.

Her unique background allows her to bridge spiritual wisdom with practical understanding of leadership, personal transformation, and conscious living. These programs continue to reach participants across multiple countries.

At the centre of Rain's teachings

True transformation begins when we remember who we truly are.

Beneath the roles we play and the identities we carry, there exists a deeper awareness — a quiet wisdom that has always been present. Through Sacred Wisdom School, Rain continues guiding individuals on the path of healing, awakening, and spiritual discovery.
